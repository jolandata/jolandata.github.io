Privacy Policy

How personal information is collected, used, and protected when you use this website.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

This website is operated by Dr. Jolanda G. Tromp (“I”, “me”, “my”). I act as the data controller for the personal data collected through this website. You can reach me for any privacy-related matter at jolanda.tromp@duytan.edu.vn.

2. What data this website collects

This is a static website. It does not run its own tracking, analytics, or advertising scripts, and it does not place tracking cookies on your device. The personal data that can reach me is limited to what you choose to provide:

  • Contact form: your name and email address, plus whatever you write in your message. Messages are sent directly to my email inbox (mailto form) and are not stored on this website.
  • Newsletter subscription (GenXR Watch): your name and email address, collected through MailerLite so I can send you the newsletter by email.
  • Membership sign-up: your name and email address, collected through MailerLite so I can send you course access details by email.

3. Automatically collected technical information

Like most websites, this site is served by a hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and device information as part of standard web delivery. This information is used for network delivery and basic security, not for profiling you.

4. How your data is used

  • To respond to your enquiries (contact form).
  • To send you the GenXR Watch newsletter you subscribed to.
  • To send you course access details for the free membership course library.
  • To maintain the security and operation of the website.

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, and I do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Legal bases for processing

  • Consent: when you subscribe to the newsletter or sign up for membership, you consent to your name and email being used for that purpose. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 8).
  • Legitimate interests: responding to your enquiries and operating this website.
  • Compliance: meeting any legal obligations that apply to me.

6. Third-party processors

  • MailerLite (mailerlite.com) — processes newsletter and membership sign-up data on my behalf, including storing your name and email and delivering email to you. MailerLite’s privacy policy applies to their processing.
  • GitHub Pages (github.com) — hosts this website and delivers its pages to your browser.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.google.com) — serves the fonts used on this site.

7. Data retention

Contact form emails are kept in my inbox like ordinary correspondence. Newsletter and membership details are kept for as long as you remain subscribed. You can ask me to delete your data at any time (see section 8), after which it is removed from my records and any processor’s records within their normal deletion cycles.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you;
  • Ask for correction of inaccurate data;
  • Ask for deletion of your data;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Receive your data in a portable form;
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (e.g. unsubscribe from the newsletter);
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email jolanda.tromp@duytan.edu.vn. Every newsletter email also includes an unsubscribe link.

9. Data security

This website is served over HTTPS, and data you submit through the sign-up forms is transmitted to MailerLite over encrypted connections. I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited personal data involved.

10. External links

This website links to external sites (for example sitenetwork.io and podcast or publication hosts). I am not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; their own policies apply once you leave this website.

11. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.

13. Contact

Questions or requests regarding privacy: jolanda.tromp@duytan.edu.vn.
