## Problem Statement

Dr. Jolanda G. Tromp is a professional in the XR/AI/human factors space — consultant, innovator, researcher. She needs a **brand website** that professionally presents her business identity, services, and value proposition to potential clients, partners, and collaborators.

Currently there is no dedicated brand website — this project fills that gap.

## Target Audience / Clients

Who will visit this site?

- **Consultancy clients** — organizations seeking XR/AI/human factors expertise
- **Research collaborators** — academic and industry partners
- **Speaking / event organizers** — conferences, panels, keynotes
- **Grant / funding bodies** — evaluating proposals and track record
- **General professional network** — LinkedIn visitors wanting more depth

## Success Criteria

What does "done" look like?

- [ ] Live website accessible at a chosen domain/URL
- [ ] Presents business identity clearly (who, what, why)
- [ ] Showcases services, expertise, and value proposition
- [ ] Includes a way for visitors to get in touch
- [ ] Professional, modern, mobile-responsive design
- [ ] Works as a marketing and credibility tool

## Scope

**In scope:**
- One-page or multi-page brand website
- Business identity (name, tagline, logo, colors)
- About / bio section
- Services / offerings section
- Portfolio or case study highlights
- Contact / inquiry mechanism
- Hosting and domain setup

**Out of scope (for now):**
- Blog / content management system
- E-commerce or payment processing
- Client portal or login area
