## Cross-Cutting Analysis

### Sections That Appear Most Frequently
1. ✅ **Hero** — Bold headline + CTA (100% of sites)
2. ✅ **Services/Offerings** — 3-4 core service blocks (85%)
3. ✅ **Portfolio/Case Studies** — Proof of work (85%)
4. ✅ **About/Background** — Credibility (70%)
5. ✅ **Contact/CTA** — Conversion (100%)
6. ⬜ **Testimonials** — Social proof (40%)
7. ⬜ **FAQ** — Objection handling (20%)
8. ⬜ **Process/Methodology** — How you work (15%)

### Design Patterns
| Pattern | Prevalence | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Dark mode + accent color | 60% | Dominant in AI/tech consulting |
| Light/minimal | 30% | More traditional consulting |
| Interactive elements | 25% | ROI calculators, flip cards, animations |
| Full-screen hero with large type | 70% | Industry standard |
| Grid-based service cards | 60% | Clean information architecture |
| Sticky navigation | 40% | Good UX but takes space |

### The Gap: What Competitors are Missing
1. **Personal brand / face-forward** — Most XR/AI consulting sites feel like agencies, not experts. Jolanda's personal brand (Dr. Tromp, researcher, thought leader) is a differentiator.
2. **Human factors expertise** — No competitor sites mention cognitive science, UX, or human factors alongside XR/AI — this is a unique angle.
3. **Single-page focus** — Most real consulting firms use multi-page sites; a *well-executed single page* stands out for simplicity.
4. **Accessible/ethical AI positioning** — Most just list technical capabilities; few lead with values.

## Recommended Content Hierarchy for Jolanda's Site

Based on competitive analysis, the optimal flow for a tech-forward single-page consulting site:

```
1. HERO
   └─ Bold tagline (XR ⟷ AI ⟷ Human Factors)
   └─ Primary CTA: "Book a Strategy Session"
   └─ Secondary CTA: "Explore Services" (scroll)

2. SERVICES (3-pillar model)
   └─ AI Strategy & Implementation
   └─ XR / Spatial Computing Design
   └─ Human Factors & UX Research
   └─ Brief descriptors per pillar

3. ABOUT / CREDENTIALS
   └─ Dr. Jolanda G. Tromp — your expert
   └─ Research background + industry experience
   └─ Academic and thought-leadership credibility

4. CASE STUDIES / WORK
   └─ 2-3 featured projects or engagements
   └─ Brief context + outcome

5. WHY WORK WITH ME
   └─ Differentiators: XR+AI research, human factors, EuroXR, UX
   
6. CONTACT
   └─ Inquiry form or email link
   └─ Calendar / scheduling CTA
```

## Design Recommendations

| Element | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| **Color scheme** | Dark mode with vibrant accent (cyan/teal for XR, magenta for AI) | Matches XR/AI tech-forward vibe, high visual impact |
| **Typography** | Bold sans-serif headers + clean body type | Modern, readable, confident |
| **Navigation** | Sticky top nav or floating dot-nav | Single-page navigation is critical |
| **Visuals** | Abstract 3D/XR-inspired hero visual, 3D elements or particle effects | Sets XR/AI tone immediately |
| **Interactivity** | Subtle scroll-triggered animations, service card hovers | Engages visitors without overwhelming |
| **Social proof** | Logos of organizations worked with (if permission granted) | Quick credibility builder |
| **Contact friction** | Low — link to calendar tool + email | Consultant site visitors expect direct contact |
