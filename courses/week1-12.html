Week 1 - Module 1: Introduction to XR-AI in Product Management

Quiz: On the Intersection of AI, XR, Blockchain, and HCI

Knowledge check

Test yourself on this module. The correct answer(s) are marked below each question.

1. Which of the following best describes how AI can be used in product development and management?

  • AI can analyze large datasets to extract valuable insights for product strategy and decision-making ✅
  • AI can create physical prototypes of products automatically
  • AI replaces product managers by automating all decision-making processes
  • AI can be used to create XR experiences but cannot be embedded in XR applications

2. How does XR technology enhance user engagement in product development?

  • XR provides immersive product demonstrations, training simulations, and interactive experiences ✅
  • XR allows users to create and sell their own digital products
  • XR can only be used for gaming and entertainment purposes
  • XR replaces the need for physical prototypes and user testing.

3. What role does Blockchain play in product ownership and management?

  • Blockchain ensures transparency and trust in supply chains by tracking and authenticating the provenance of products ✅
  • Blockchain is primarily used for creating 3D models
  • Blockchain technology is used to enhance the graphics in XR applications
  • Blockchain solely focuses on financial transactions and cannot be applied to product management

4. Which of the following best describes the importance of HCI in product ownership?

  • HCI principles ensure that products are intuitive, accessible, and user-friendly ✅
  • HCI replaces the need for user feedback in product development
  • HCI is only concerned with the technical specifications of a product
  • HCI methods are irrelevant in the context of emerging technologies like AI and XR
