Plethora of Prototyping Tools

There are many digital tools that can be used to create XR-AI apps, and each application comes with it's own learning curve. 

Our approach is to try everything and learn a little bit about the UI and learn to experiment what the tools can be used for, stretching the edges of what is technically feasible to search for the cutting edge of creative solutions and innovation.

Here are some currently popular tools. Keep in mind that with the advent of GenAI there is huge and rapid increase in the types of tools available, and what is new today may be old tomorrow!

The good news is that GenAI can help us understand and troubleshoot a lot of the digital tools, so we can get much further in our learning, much faster. It is worth remembering that being proficient with many of the larger software platforms such as Unity, Unreal Engine, or Photoshop, to name a few, can easily take 10.000 hours (2 years) of dedicated study to reach junior-level skills. We must all start by learning somewhere, sometime.
