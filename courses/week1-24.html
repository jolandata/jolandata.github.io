Week 1 - Module 1: Introduction to XR-AI in Product Management

Assignment 1B: Research and Analyze a Product that Integrates Emerging Technologies

Assignment 1B:

  • Research and analyze three products that integrate at least two or more of the mentioned technologies.
  • Decide on a new product proposal of your own, that combines as many of the technologies discussed.
  • Create a PPT presentation with a minimum of 4 slides per product, for the three existing products you analyzed.
  • Highlight for each product:
    • the features,
    • the target market, and
    • the competitive advantages.

Submit your presentation in the Assignments section, under Assignment 1B.
