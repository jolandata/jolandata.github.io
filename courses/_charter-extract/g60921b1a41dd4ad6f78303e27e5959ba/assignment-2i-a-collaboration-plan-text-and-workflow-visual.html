9. Cross-Functional Collaboration

Exercise: Collaboration and Teamwork Documentation

Steps:

1. Collaboration Plan:
   - Open a word processor.
   - Write a 300-word plan on how to ensure effective collaboration. Example format:
     - Regular Communication: [How often will you communicate and through what channels?]
     - Conflict Resolution: [How will you handle conflicts?]
     - Collaborative Culture: [How will you foster a collaborative environment?]

2. Collaboration Workflow:
   - Use a tool like Flowchart Maker or any drawing tool.
   - Create a visual workflow showing the collaboration process.
   - Include stages such as ideation, development, testing, and review.

Deliverable: A collaboration plan (text) and workflow (visual).
