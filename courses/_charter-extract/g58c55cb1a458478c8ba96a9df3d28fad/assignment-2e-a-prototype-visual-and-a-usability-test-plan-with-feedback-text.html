5. Development and Iteration

Exercise: Prototyping and Usability Testing

Steps:

1. Create Prototype:
   - Use a tool like Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD to design a low-fidelity prototype.
     - Focus on one feature of your product.
     - Create basic screens and interactions to demonstrate how the feature works.

2. Usability Test Plan:
   - Open a word processor.
   - Write a 200-word usability test plan. Example format:
     - Objective: [What are you testing?]
     - Target Users: [Who will test the prototype?]
     - Tasks: [What tasks will users perform?]
     - Metrics: [How will you measure success? (e.g., time to complete tasks, user satisfaction)]

3. Conduct Usability Test:
   - Find at least two people to test your prototype.
   - Observe them as they complete the tasks and take notes on their feedback.

Deliverable: A prototype (visual) and a usability test plan with feedback (text).
