The Importance of the XR-AI Product Manager Role

The XR-AI Product Manager plays a critical role in ensuring the success of XR products. With the advancement of AI tools for 3D development, the creation of XR application content and functionalities is becoming easier, requiring less coding and time investment. However, the creative aspect remains crucial, as human product managers are essential for:
- Inventing new interaction paradigms and innovative ways to use XR.
- Ensuring incremental prototype developments are thoroughly tested with representative end-users.
- Achieving ethical, safe, high-precision, and effective remote collaboration with high ROI.
