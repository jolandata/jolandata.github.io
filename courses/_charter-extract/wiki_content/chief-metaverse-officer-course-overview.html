Here is the overview of the course contents:

 

Course Title: Chief Metaverse Officer Course: Converging Technologies in Product Management & Ownership: "AI, XR, Blockchain, and HCI"

 

Course Duration: 4 weeks/4 modules (at your own pace)

Module 1: ~8 hours coursework, coaching online 50 minutes (complementary), prototype ideation 4+ hours.

Module 2: ~8 hours coursework, prototype building and testing 4+ hours.

Module 3: ~8 hours coursework,  50 minutes coaching online (optional), prototype refinement 4+ hours.

Module 4: ~4.5 hours coursework, Portfolio & Pitch preparation time 4+ hours,  50 minutes coaching online (optional).

 

Course Overview:
This course will explore the intersection of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Extended Reality (XR), Blockchain, and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) within the context of product management / product ownership and the roles of a Chief Metaverse Officer. A Chief Metaverse Officer (CMO) is a strategic leadership role within an organization responsible for guiding its presence, innovation, and operations within the metaverse — a collection of immersive digital environments and platforms built with technologies like XR, Web3, blockchain, AI, and spatial computing.

Participants will gain a comprehensive understanding of how these technologies influence product development and management processes. Throughout the course, the emphasis will be on the practical use of the Chief Metaverse Officer: Product Manager/Product Owner/HCI Expert/MBA skills to present product proposals and product portfolio development for efficient teamwork and product management. This will empower participants to showcase their skills as product owners in a competitive job market with a personalized product proposal & pitch.

 

  • Week 1: Introduction to Emerging Technologies for XR-AI Product Management / Product Ownership
    - Overview of AI, XR, Blockchain, and HCI
    - Understanding the impact of these new and converging technologies on product management / product ownership
    - Case studies and real-world examples
    - Assignments
    • 1A: Reflective essay on the role of emerging technologies in product management / product ownership
    • 1B: Review three Products
    • 1C: Put together your own Product Proposal using Two Tools from the Product Manager / Product Owner Toolkit

 

  • Week 2: Agile & Lean XR-AI Product Management in the Age of Disruption
    - Agile & Lean XR-AI principles and methodologies.
    - Integrating emerging technologies into the agile & lean XR-AI product development process.
    - Hands-on exercises in developing agile & lean XR-AI product proposals using product management/owner tools.
    - Assignments:
    • 2A: Develop a product roadmap integrating AI, XR, Blockchain, and HCI elements using Product Manager / Product Owner Tools.
    • 2B: Build and test your own Product Prototype (Iterative Low-fidelity/Mid-fidelity/High-fidelity development process).
    • 2C: Improve the low/mid-fidelity Prototype based on User Feedback, Repeat Build-test (2B-2C).

 

  • Week 3: Designing User-Centric Experiences with Emerging Technologies
    - Apply the Principles of user-centered XR-AI design
    - Leverage AI, XR, Blockchain, and HCI for enhanced user experiences
    - Prototyping and user testing techniques
    - Assignment:
    • 3A: Create a mid/high-fidelity prototype demonstrating user-centric XR-AI design principles.
    • 3B: Test the mid/high fidelity prototype with end-users.
    • 3C: Improve the low/mid-fidelity Prototype based on User Feedback, Repeat Build-test (2B-2C).

 

  • Week 4: Building a Personalized Professional Brand as a Product Manager / Product Owner
    - Crafting a compelling CV and portfolio
    - Personal branding strategies for job seekers
    - Networking and job search techniques
    - Assignment:
    • 4A: Finish the prototype and portfolio.
    • 4B: Develop a personalized professional marketing plan highlighting your skills and expertise as an XR-AI product manager/owner.
    • 4C: Present your XR-AI Product Manager/Owner Pitch and Portfolio for Interviews

 

Course Deliverables:
1. Reflective essay on the role of emerging technologies in product management / product ownership to identofy your own prefered niche.

2. Product proposal and roadmap integrating AI, XR, Blockchain, or HCI elements, using Product Manager's Tools.

3. Create low-fidelity, mid-fidelity, and high-fidelity product prototypes that demonstrate your product developer skills, knowledge of user-centric design principles, and Product Manager skills.

4. Your XR-AI Product Manager/Owner Portfolio; Personalized professional marketing plan (CV, portfolio of your XR-AI Course Assignments and Prototypes, your professional pitch, and your personal Product Manager branding strategy).

 

Assessments:
Participants will be assessed based on the completion of quizzes, the quality of their assignments, and their pro-active participation in preparing their own professional pitch and portfolio. When completing all assignments of good quality, you will receive a social media badge, and a certificate of attendance.

 

Outcome:
By the end of the course, participants will have developed a comprehensive portfolio showcasing their skills and expertise as product owners in the realm of emerging technologies. They will also have a personalized professional marketing plan aimed at making them irresistible job candidates to potential employers in the tech industry.
