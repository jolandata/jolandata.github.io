2. Stakeholder Engagement and Communication

Exercise: Stakeholder Mapping and Communication Plan

Steps:

1. Stakeholder Map:
   - Open a drawing tool like Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Drawings, or an online tool like Lucidchart.
   - Draw two axes: Influence (Low to High) on the x-axis and Interest (Low to High) on the y-axis.
   - Identify potential stakeholders (e.g., users, developers, investors, etc.) and place them on the map based on their influence and interest.
   - Label each stakeholder group.

2. Communication Plan:
   - Open a word processor.
   - Write a 200-word communication plan. Example format:
     - Stakeholder Group: [Stakeholder Group Name]
     - Communication Method: [How will you communicate? (e.g., email, meetings)]
     - Frequency: [How often will you communicate? (e.g., weekly, monthly)]
     - Purpose: [Why are you communicating? (e.g., updates, feedback)]

Deliverable: A stakeholder map (visual) and a communication plan (text).
