Course Welcome Information

Introduction to your course leader

 

“Hi, I’m Dr. Jolanda G. Tromp — your guide into the immersive unknown. I believe every XR prototype should start with empathy... and good coffee. When I’m not mentoring future Chief XR Officers, you’ll find me roaming the internet to explore the cutting edge of immersive tech — or hiking the foothills of the Sierra Nevada to test edge cases (literally). Let’s build the future with curiosity, creativity, and purpose.”

 

I am so excited that you have joined me and the other participants on this course, and for our journey together and watching you develop your ideas!

I am Chief Metaverse Officer of the Center for Visualization and Simulation, at the Computer Science department of the Duy Tan University in Da Nang, Vietnam. I am also assistant professor at the State University of New York in Oswego, NY, USA. I am a consultant, mentor, and teacher online, via my own company, Global Teamwork 3D. 

 

I live in Spain, and I receive online and local clients, interns, and students for XR research projects to specialize as XR and Metaverse experts, and fast-track their portfolio development and career orientation.
