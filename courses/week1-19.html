Week 1 - Module 1: Introduction to XR-AI in Product Management

Quiz: Test your understanding of the role of XR-AI Product Manager

Knowledge check

Test yourself on this module. The correct answer(s) are marked below each question.

1. What is a key activity in user research and feedback analysis for an XR-AI Product Manager?

  • Conducting user research to understand target users' needs, preferences, and behaviors ✅
  • Developing features based only on personal assumptions
  • Ignoring usability testing results and focusing on initial design concepts
  • Relying solely on quantitative data for user feedback

2. How should an XR-AI Product Manager approach product launch and go-to-market strategy?

  • Coordinate with marketing and sales teams to create marketing materials and promotional campaigns ✅
  • Develop go-to-market strategies without involving the marketing team
  • Launch the product without any promotional activities
  • Focus solely on pricing and ignore distribution strategies

3. Which of the following best describes the role of an XR-AI Product Manager in product strategy and roadmapping?

  • Develop product roadmaps that detail the features, functionalities, and enhancements over time based on market analysis, user research, and business goals ✅
  • Define the vision and strategic direction for XR products based solely on technical specifications
  • Prioritize product initiatives based on internal team preferences
  • Ignore market trends and user feedback when planning product features

4. How does an XR-AI Product Manager facilitate cross-functional collaboration?

  • By acting as a liaison between stakeholders and conveying their requirements and expectations to the development team ✅
  • By solely working with engineers and ignoring other teams
  • By making all product decisions independently without team input
  • By limiting communication to formal meetings only
